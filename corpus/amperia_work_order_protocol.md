# Amperia Work Order and Service Window Protocol

## Intake and Validation
Each work order must be reviewed before dispatch. The technician verifies the customer name, service address, asset type, issue summary, promised service window, and any special instructions. A service call without a valid asset tag or serial number may still be attempted, but the technician must document any missing information and request a follow-up before closing the job.

## Service Window Rules
Amperia guarantees a response window only when the job is within the active service plan and access is available. If the issue is outside the active maintenance window, the task may require manager review, customer approval, or a rescheduling recommendation. The technician may not assume the job is covered by a warranty unless the asset record and contract status are confirmed.

## Approval Paths
Any recommended repair, replacement, or special handling must be included in the work order with an explanation of why the scope exceeds the original call. The customer must approve nonstandard work before the technician executes it, unless the company policy requires emergency mitigation to prevent further damage or hazards.

## Required Findings
The technician must record the original customer complaint, observed symptoms, diagnostic checks, parts inspected, measurement values, and final disposition. If the unit cannot be restored during the visit, the note must state whether the issue is a temporary fix, a deferred repair, or a failed inspection.

## Closeout Standards
Closing a work order without documenting the resolution, customer communication, and next steps is not permitted. A customer signature is required for work that changes the system or installs a new component. If a signature cannot be obtained, the technician must record the reason, notify dispatch, and request the appropriate escalation path.

## Escalation Trigger
Escalation is required when the task has a safety risk, lacks owner access, exceeds the current service contract, or requires a different skill set or inventory item beyond the current technician's approved scope.
