# Amperia Parts and Inventory Handling Rules

## Parts Authorization
Technicians may only install parts that are approved for the model and service code on the work order. If a required part is not available in the current vehicle inventory or the customer site does not support the intended repair, the technician must notify dispatch and document the reason before using substitute or nonstandard materials.

## Inventory Verification
Before leaving for service, technicians confirm that the required tools, consumables, and critical replacement parts are stocked for the route. If the issue is caused by a failed capacitor, condenser fan motor, or compressor component, the technician must check compatibility with the model serial number and voltage requirements to avoid incorrect replacement.

## Defect and Return Rule
If a replacement part is installed and later fails, the technician must record the original failure, the replaced component identifier, and the installation sequence. This supports warranty claims and prevents duplicate replacement based on vendor or route mismatch.

## Shared Inventory with Dispatch
Inventory decisions are not made in isolation. Dispatch can authorize cross-vehicle transfers, special order parts, or different models when the active job conditions, customer access, or site constraints prevent a standard fix. The technician remains responsible for documenting the reason and obtaining approval before the swap occurs.

## Final Parts Documentation
Every job must record the component replaced, part number, condition found, whether the failure was electrical, mechanical, or airflow related, and whether any customer approval was required. This ensures the next technician or specialist has a complete inventory and service history for the asset.
